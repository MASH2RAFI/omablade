# Configure blade behavior

Open the settings panel from the blade gear. It is split into General, Layout, Module settings, Desktop integration, and About & reset; click a heading, or Tab to it and press Enter, to fold it open or shut. Searching shows matching rows from every section.

```sh
omablade blade settings left
```
